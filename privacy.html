Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-21

In short: the game runs entirely in your browser. It has no accounts, no sign-up and no forms, and it doesn’t send what you type or guess to me. The only data collection comes from whether or not you have closed the "How to play" popup, the hosting service and, if enabled, the advertising described in section 3.

1. Who runs this site

This site (“Drug Guesser”) is operated by LGLmage. You can contact me at lglmage@proton.me with any privacy question or request.

2. What the game itself does

  • Your guesses, the text you type and your results stay in your browser. They are not sent to me or stored on a server.
  • The daily puzzle is chosen using the date shown on your device. The date is used on your device only.
  • The game only stores whether or not you have seen the "how to play" screen.

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites, and to measure how ads perform. Depending on where you are, ads may be personalised or non-personalised.

  • Google’s use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners show ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites.
  • You can manage or opt out of personalised advertising in Google’s ad settings at adssettings.google.com, or through optout.aboutads.info.
  • You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or another region that requires consent, you will be asked for your choice through a consent banner before personalised ads or cookies are used. You can change your choice at any time using the banner’s settings.

I do not control the cookies set by advertisers and have no access to the information they collect.

4. Hosting and server logs

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub may automatically collect technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, as described in GitHub’s Privacy Statement. I do not receive or analyse this data.

5. Analytics

The number of users that pass through the site is recorded. This is the only analytical tool used.

6. Links to other sites

The site may link to other websites, such as PsychonautWiki. I’m not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please check their own policies.

7. Children

This site covers psychoactive substances and is intended for adults. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK, or Canadian privacy law), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because the game itself holds no personal information about you, most requests would concern the advertising partners or GitHub, which you can contact directly. You can also contact me at the address above and I will help where I can.

9. Educational content

This site is for education and entertainment. Its data is simplified for gameplay and may contain errors. It is not medical advice or drug-use advice. For accurate information, see sources such as psychonautwiki.org and speak to a health professional.
